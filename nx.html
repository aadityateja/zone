16 // EPISTEMIC INTEGRITY & QUALITY CONTROL

The Scientific Rigour Spectrum

Creativity must never blur into pseudoscience. For every project, statement, and visual metaphor, we enforce clear cognitive demarcation.

01. Established Science

Empirically verified, peer-reviewed physical laws (e.g. Maxwell's equations, general relativity in weak fields, QED).

02. Active Research

Ongoing experimental inquiries with competing models (e.g. neutrino mass hierarchy, dark matter candidates, EIC hadronic showers).

03. Hypothesis

Mathematically consistent proposals awaiting falsifiable empirical tests (e.g. sterile neutrinos, axions, modified gravity).

04. Speculation

Theoretical extrapolations at boundaries of knowledge (e.g. multiverse topologies, holographic principle, wormholes).

05. Metaphor

Analogies used to build intuition (e.g. rubber sheet spacetime, particle wave packets as vibrating strings). Always explicitly labeled.

06. Artistic Interpretation

Sensory, emotional, or philosophical resonance inspired by science without claiming physical equivalence.
