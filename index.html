Before we talk about school
WHAT
IS
A CLOUD?
Look up.

A cloud looks like an object. But it isn't one.

It is a dynamic system of countless tiny water droplets and ice crystals moving through a constantly changing atmosphere.
Atmospheric physics
A cloud is not a thing floating in the sky. It is a process happening inside the atmosphere.
01 / WHY IS IT UP?

It isn't weightless.

Water has mass. Clouds have mass. But individual droplets are incredibly small. Air currents, turbulence and buoyancy can keep them suspended while gravity continually pulls them downward.

02 / WHY DOESN'T IT FALL?

Because the system moves.

Droplets are constantly falling, evaporating, growing and being replaced. Rising air can carry tiny droplets upward while evaporation removes others. The cloud stays visible because the process continuously renews itself.

03 / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE?

Everything is moving.

Condensation. Evaporation. Updrafts. Downdrafts. Ice formation. Collisions. Charge separation. A cloud is an active physical environment.
