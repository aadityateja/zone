ASYMMETRY / 001 THE EXPERIMENT
An experiment in thought

THE EXAM

One room. One person. One question.
Nothing to copy. Nowhere to look.

SCROLL TO BEGIN

Examination / 001

You are the only student.

A classroom surrounds you. Every table is empty except yours. A camera watches from each corner. There is a clock on the wall. On your desk, a sheet of paper.

The speaker activates.

"You may begin."

Question 01 / Physics

Consider a physical system whose complete state is unknown. What can you infer from the observations available to you?

Show your reasoning. State your assumptions. You may begin anywhere.

Phase II / Memory

The question opens a door.

You pick up the pen. An equation appears in your mind. Then a classroom. A forgotten derivation. A mistake. Something a teacher once said.

Your memories are not answers. They are places you can search.

Select a memory. Let it change the room.

Phase III / Observation

Who is observing whom?

You look at the camera. You wonder what it records. Your movements. Your hesitation. The answer on your paper.

Can it measure your understanding? Can it see the thought that never becomes a word?

Move your cursor. The geometry responds to you. The visualization is a metaphor, not a literal quantum measurement experiment.

Final phase / Submission

The clock stops.

You have an answer. Perhaps it is correct. Perhaps it isn't. You put down the pen.

For the first time, the room is completely still.
